Manogna

Researcher – Computer Vision & Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

Manogna develops computer vision pipelines for early BRD detection in beef cattle and contributes to the ViCBio DM agent-based model for circular dairy bioeconomy.

Projects

Computer Vision for Hybrid Intelligent Modeling of BRD

A computer vision pipeline that detects and segments each cow in video, tracks it individually, and monitors behaviors like feeding, standing, and walking over time to catch early signs of illness by comparing each animal to its own baseline rather than the herd average. Fuses continuous visual identification with independent Ceres Tag sensor data so no single signal is a point of failure.

Strategic Analytics for Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

Contributed to ViCBio DM, an agent-based virtual farm modeling nutrient, water, energy, and by-product loops for sustainable milk production, alongside Dr. Megha Poyyara Saiju.
