Serinmary Pulikkottil Rejimon

Doctoral Researcher – Sustainable Livestock Production Systems

Serin is a Ph.D. researcher in Animal Science at Texas A&M University, where she uses modeling to improve the sustainability of livestock production systems. Her work integrates artificial intelligence, system dynamics, and economic analysis to evaluate strategies for reducing methane emissions and enhancing production efficiency.

By leveraging data from precision livestock technologies, she supports informed decision-making in beef systems. Serin is also passionate about mentoring students and contributing to science-based agricultural policy.

Projects

Assessing Sustainable Beef Production Practices in the U.S.

A critical review and multi-criteria decision-making (MCDA) approach assessing animal and resource management practices for sustainable beef production, published in Agricultural Systems and presented at the 2025 ASAS Annual Meeting.

Machine Learning for Predictive Analytics of Beef Cattle Performance

Establishing machine learning models on high-dimensional data (895 data points spanning 1984–2023) to predict methane yield from animal metabolic status, measurement technique, and feed composition. Extended to climate-smart beef production in South Africa under a Climate-Change AI grant, with publications in the Journal of Cleaner Production and Sustainability Science.

System Dynamics Model of Sustainable Beef Production

A system dynamics model linking feed management, animal health, genetics, manure and soil management, water management, policy, and carbon economy loops, published in the System Dynamics Review and presented at SDS 2025 and the TSCRA Annual Meeting.

Hybrid Intelligent Mechanistic Model

A hybrid intelligent mechanistic model for methane predictions in beef cattle, including a computer vision component for behavior classification in confined dairy cattle (Frontiers in Animal Science; Computers and Electronics in Agriculture).

Mentors undergraduate researchers through the Aggie Collaborate program.
