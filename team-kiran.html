Projects

Agentic RAG Chatbot for Digitized INRMPs

Develops a retrieval-augmented generation chatbot enabling semantic querying across digitized Integrated Natural Resources Management Plans (INRMPs) for the NRI project, grounding every response in cited sources to accelerate standardization and retrofitting workflows.

Computer Vision for Hybrid Intelligent Modeling of BRD (CVDS)

Contributes programming support to the lab's computer vision decision-support pipeline for early BRD detection — detecting, segmenting, and tracking individual cattle to flag early behavioral signs of illness.

Digital Twin for Methane Emissions Monitoring

Contributes to a real-time digital twin integrating sensor data and agent-based modeling for predicting methane emissions.

Kiran also builds and maintains this lab website, including the automated Google Scholar publications sync and photo gallery.
