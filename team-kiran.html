Kiran Babu Athina

Research Technician – Data Management & LLM Development, NRI Project

Kiran is a Research Technician in the Department of Animal Science, developing data management strategies and large language model tools for the NRI project, and contributing programming support to digital twin initiatives across the lab.

He also builds and maintains this lab website, including the automated Google Scholar publications sync and photo gallery.

Projects

Agentic RAG Chatbot for Digitized INRMPs

Develops a retrieval-augmented generation chatbot enabling semantic querying across digitized Integrated Natural Resources Management Plans (INRMPs) for the NRI project, grounding every response in cited sources to accelerate standardization and retrofitting workflows.

Digital Twin Framework for Energy Partitioning and Growth Optimization in Beef Cattle

Contributes to a 3D digital twin of feedlot cattle and pen infrastructure, simulating energy partitioning and growth optimization for beef cattle systems.
