Research Products

Innovative AI solutions for sustainable livestock systems

Our Research Portfolio

Agent Based Model of Bovine Respiratory Disease

ABM models the beef supply chain dynamics based on attributes and decision rules for BRD prediction.

Intelligent Bovine Respiratory Disease Management System

Data-driven intelligent decision support and management system for Bovine Respiratory Disease.

Cow Calf Operation Visualizer

Interactive visualization tool for monitoring and analyzing cow-calf operations in real-time.

Digital Twin for Methane Emissions Monitoring

Real-time digital twin integrating sensor data and ABM for predicting methane emissions.

Feedlot Economics Decision Visualization Tool

Economic scenario simulation tool for feedlot operations and investment optimization.

Machine Learning Price Prediction for Cow-Calf Operations

ARIMA-based intelligent decision support system for U.S. cow-calf price prediction.

Methane Yield Prediction Model

Machine learning models for predicting methane yield from feed database for carbon credit tracking.

Economic Decision-Making for Texas Stocker Cattle

Machine learning-based forecasting tool for Texas stocker cattle operations.

System Dynamics Model of Sustainable Beef Management

Comprehensive system dynamics modeling for sustainable beef management practices.

ViCBio DM: An Agent-Based Model for Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

An agent-based virtual dairy farm linking nutrient, water, biogas energy, and by-product loops through an AI-enabled decision support system.

INRMP Standardisation & Retro-fitting

A repeatable AI-assisted methodology for converting existing Integrated Natural Resources Management Plans into a standardised eight-chapter template.

An Intelligent RAG Chatbot for INRMP Standardization

An agentic retrieval-augmented generation chatbot for semantic querying across digitized INRMPs, grounding every response in cited sources.

Optimizing Dairy Cattle Sustainability and Profitability Through the EcoS Index

Genomic selection simulation modeling feeding a web-based decision tool for comparing beef, conventional, and sexed semen breeding strategies.

AI in Veterinary and Animal Science: A Book Project

A 15-chapter book on principles, applications, and future directions, authored by the AISLS Lab for Elsevier's ELSA platform.

SubAcute and Acute Ruminal Acidosis (SAARA) in Feedlot Cattle: A Multifactorial Review

A structured review of the environmental, animal, management, and nutritional factors driving SAARA in feedlot cattle, linking rapid diet transitions and high-starch rations to rumen damage and reduced feed conversion.

Advanced Calf Price Forecasting for U.S. Cow-Calf Operations

Multivariate ML/DL models (Random Forest, AdaBoost, SVR, LSTM) with walk-forward validation forecast calf prices at 1-, 3-, and 6-month horizons using exogenous demand, supply, and macroeconomic signals.

Data-Driven Stocker Calf Price Forecasting for Texas Cattle Operations

Weekly Texas stocker auction price data combined with exogenous market variables forecast prices at 1-, 4-, and 13-week horizons, supporting buy/sell timing for Texas stocker operations.

System Dynamics Approach to Cow-Calf Profitability

A causal loop model linking herd reproduction, genetic improvement, feed costs, market prices, and disease outbreak risk through reinforcing and balancing feedback loops.

Feedlot Economics and Management: A Visualization Tool for Decision Support

An interactive feedlot pricing calculator computing break-even price, profit per head, and rate of gain from head count, weights, and cattle futures prices.

Digital Twin Framework for Energy Partitioning and Growth Optimization in Beef Cattle

A 3D digital twin of feedlot cattle and pen infrastructure built to simulate energy partitioning and growth optimization for beef cattle systems.

Epidemiology of Bovine Respiratory Disease (BRD)

A published review of BRD epidemiology informing mitigation strategies in U.S. beef production (Adekunle & Kaniyamattam, 2026, Agriculture, 16(3), 311).

System Dynamics Modeling of BRD

Causal loop diagrams, stock-and-flow modeling, and scenario analysis of the epidemiological, antimicrobial, and economic implications of BRD in U.S. beef production.

Agent-Based Modeling of BRD

Agent-based simulation of BRD spread and control decisions across a beef production system.

Beef Production Economic Visualization Tool

An AI-powered decision support dashboard for visualizing the economics of beef production.

Digital Twin of the Beef Cattle System

An intuitive dashboard for the digital twin architecture of the Texas A&M Animal Science Beef Cattle Systems Center.

Visualizable BRD Decision Support Dashboard

An AI-powered decision support dashboard for early prediction and control of BRD.

Assessing Sustainable Beef Production Practices in the U.S.

A critical review and multi-criteria decision-making (MCDA) approach assessing animal and resource management practices for sustainable beef production.

Machine Learning for Predictive Analytics of Beef Cattle Performance

Machine learning models on high-dimensional data predict methane yield from animal metabolic status, measurement technique, and feed composition, extended to climate-smart beef production in South Africa.

System Dynamics Model of Sustainable Beef Production

A system dynamics model linking feed management, animal health, genetics, manure and soil management, water management, policy, and carbon economy loops.

Hybrid Intelligent Mechanistic Model

A hybrid intelligent mechanistic model for methane predictions in beef cattle, including a computer vision component for behavior classification in confined dairy cattle.

System Dynamics Model for the Water-Energy-Food Nexus in Dairy Farms

A whole-herd system dynamics model integrating water and energy modules with an existing dairy herd lifecycle model, optimizing water use, energy use, and milk production together.

Agent-Based Model of the Water-Energy-Food Nexus

Represents each cow as an autonomous decision-making agent with individual water, feed, and energy needs, so farm-level resource use emerges bottom-up from agent interactions.

Cost-Benefit Economics of Resource Use Optimization

A web-based decision tool quantifying the return on investment and payback period of water/energy-efficiency technologies for Texas dairy farms.

Life Cycle Assessment of Texan Dairy Resource Flows

A cradle-to-farm-gate LCA quantifying total water and energy flows to support Dairy Net Zero roadmap reporting and carbon/water footprint credentialing.

System Dynamics Model for Foot-and-Mouth Disease

A system dynamics SEIRC framework simulating Foot-and-Mouth Disease spread and control, linking epidemiological dynamics to on-farm production and resource-use decisions.

Cross-Country Dairy Financial Resilience Benchmarking

A Financial Resilience Index (FRI) benchmarking U.S., German, and New Zealand dairy farms using IFCN TIPI-CAL data, tracing resilience gaps to liquidity and balance-sheet structure.

Farmer-Preference Strategy Ranking (MCDA / COPRAS)

Turns farmer priorities into a defensible strategy ranking using ROC weighting and COPRAS across a stratified sample of dairy farms.

Visual Analytics Dashboard for Farm Decision-Making

Translates financial data into Power BI and R Shiny dashboards for farmers, extension advisors, and lenders using progressive-disclosure narrative frameworks.

Computer Vision for Hybrid Intelligent Modeling of BRD

A computer vision pipeline that detects, segments, and tracks each cow's behavior over time to catch early signs of illness, fused with independent sensor data.

Strategic Analytics for Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

ViCBio DM, an agent-based virtual farm modeling nutrient, water, energy, and by-product loops for sustainable milk production.

Research Impact

Economic Optimization

Our models help livestock producers make data-driven decisions that improve profitability while maintaining sustainability.

Environmental Sustainability

Advanced monitoring and prediction systems support climate-smart cattle production strategies.

Disease Management

AI-powered surveillance and prediction tools enhance animal health outcomes and reduce disease impact.

Precision Agriculture

Integration of sensor technologies and machine learning provides real-time insights for optimal livestock management.

Supply Chain Optimization

Agent-based models simulate complex interactions across the beef value chain for improved decision-making.

Policy Support

Our research findings contribute to evidence-based agricultural policy development and implementation.

Our Research Methodology

1

Data Collection & Analysis

We gather field and empirical data from various sources and apply statistical and machine learning models for predictive analysis.

2

Complex Systems Modeling

Herd-level decision models undergo statistical analysis to investigate economic, environmental, and epidemiological performance under different management strategies.

3

Decision Support Tools

We develop customizable, dynamic, and visualizable decision tools specifically designed for livestock systems stakeholders.
