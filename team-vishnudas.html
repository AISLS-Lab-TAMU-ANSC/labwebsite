Vishnudas Kulangara Veettil

Doctoral Researcher – Agent-Based Modeling, Machine Learning for Beef Value Chain

Vishnu is a Ph.D. student in the Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Livestock Systems lab at Texas A&M University. He holds a BVSc & AH from Kerala Veterinary and Animal Sciences University, an MVSc in Livestock Economics from ICAR–Indian Veterinary Research Institute, and two postgraduate diplomas in Small Animal Theriogenology and Infectious Disease Management.

His research combines system dynamics, agent-based modeling, and machine learning to study economic and biological interactions in the U.S. beef value chain, with a focus on modeling profitability, market dynamics, animal health impacts, and forecasting cattle prices, and developing blockchain-enabled traceability and digital twin frameworks for decision support.

Projects

SubAcute and Acute Ruminal Acidosis (SAARA) in Feedlot Cattle: A Multifactorial Review

A structured review of the environmental, animal, management, and nutritional factors driving SAARA in feedlot cattle, linking rapid diet transitions and high-starch rations to rumen damage, lameness, reduced weight gain, and poor feed conversion.

Advanced Calf Price Forecasting for U.S. Cow-Calf Operations

Multivariate machine learning and deep learning models (Random Forest, AdaBoost, SVR, LSTM) with walk-forward validation forecast calf prices at 1-, 3-, and 6-month horizons using exogenous demand, supply, and macroeconomic signals. SHAP feature attribution identifies fed cattle price and drought conditions as key drivers, informing marketing, breeding, and liquidation decisions.

Data-Driven Stocker Calf Price Forecasting for Texas Cattle Operations

Weekly Texas stocker auction price data combined with exogenous variables (corn, crude oil, exchange rates, drought index) feed the same model suite at 1-, 4-, and 13-week horizons, supporting buy/sell timing and price risk management for Texas stocker operations.

System Dynamics Approach to Economic & Biological Factors Influencing Profitability in U.S. Cow-Calf Operations

A system dynamics causal loop model linking herd reproduction, genetic improvement, feed costs, market prices, and disease outbreak risk through five reinforcing and six balancing feedback loops, explaining the drivers of profitability in cow-calf operations.

Feedlot Economics and Management: A Visualization Tool for Decision Support

An interactive feedlot pricing calculator computing break-even price, profit per head, total revenue, and rate of gain from inputs like head count, start/finish weights, and cattle futures prices, with dedicated panels for feed, labor, and other cost drivers.

Digital Twin Framework for Energy Partitioning and Growth Optimization in Beef Cattle

A 3D digital twin of feedlot cattle and pen infrastructure — including cattle body modeling, UV texture mapping, and pen layout blueprints — built to simulate energy partitioning and growth optimization for beef cattle systems.
