Projects

Epidemiology of Bovine Respiratory Disease (BRD)

A published review of BRD epidemiology informing mitigation strategies in U.S. beef production (Adekunle & Kaniyamattam, 2026, Agriculture, 16(3), 311).

System Dynamics Modeling of BRD

Causal loop diagrams, stock-and-flow modeling, and scenario analysis of the epidemiological, antimicrobial, and economic implications of BRD in U.S. beef production — the basis of two ongoing peer-reviewed papers and multiple conference presentations.

Agent-Based Modeling of BRD

Agent-based simulation of BRD spread and control decisions across a beef production system.

Beef Production Economic Visualization Tool

An AI-powered decision support dashboard for visualizing the economics of beef production.

Digital Twin of the Beef Cattle System

An intuitive dashboard for the digital twin architecture of the Texas A&M Animal Science Beef Cattle Systems Center.

Visualizable BRD Decision Support Dashboard

An AI-powered decision support dashboard for early prediction and control of BRD.

Also active in grant contributions (15 total, 4 funded), undergraduate mentorship through the Aggie Collaborate program, and multiple award-winning student presentations.
