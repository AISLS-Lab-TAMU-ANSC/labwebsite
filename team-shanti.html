Shanti Kinjangi

Dairy Resilience Lab – Research Collaborator

Shanti works on the Dairy Resilience Lab initiative, benchmarking dairy farm financial resilience across the U.S., Germany, and New Zealand, and building farmer-facing decision-support dashboards, with funding from USDA NIFA (Award 2023-08095).

Projects

Cross-Country Dairy Financial Resilience Benchmarking

Built a Financial Resilience Index (FRI) benchmarking U.S., German, and New Zealand dairy farms using IFCN TIPI-CAL data. All three systems showed strong repayment capacity and profitability — the resilience gap traced mainly to liquidity and balance-sheet structure rather than farm scale (U.S. scored 1.00, Germany 0.88, New Zealand 0.67).

Farmer-Preference Strategy Ranking (MCDA / COPRAS)

Turns farmer priorities into a defensible strategy ranking using ROC weighting and COPRAS across a 45-farm stratified sample spanning Texas, California, and Wisconsin, comparing five candidate strategies.

Visual Analytics Dashboard for Farm Decision-Making

Translates financial data into Power BI and R Shiny dashboards for farmers, extension advisors, and lenders, using progressive-disclosure narrative frameworks so financial risk is understandable without spreadsheet literacy.

Research supported by USDA NIFA (Award 2023-08095).
