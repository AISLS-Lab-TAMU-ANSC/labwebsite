Dr. Megha Poyyara Saiju

Post Doctoral Research Associate

Dr. Megha Poyyara Saiju (DVM, MVSc, PhD, PGTMD) is a veterinary science researcher specialised in nutritional physiology and functional food production. Her doctoral work explored the estrogenic impact of soybean isoflavones on ovarian receptor dynamics in layer chickens, and she pioneered a safe and effective method to produce vitamin D3- and zinc-enriched designer eggs.

She brings over five years of experience in analytical and molecular techniques from a NABL-accredited Central Instruments Laboratory, and now contributes to multidisciplinary AI and decision-modeling initiatives at the AISLS Lab, spanning circular dairy bioeconomy modeling, natural resources plan standardization, genomic selection strategy, and scientific authorship.

Projects

ViCBio DM: An Agent-Based Model for Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

An agent-based virtual dairy farm linking nutrient management, water recycling, biogas energy, and dairy by-product loops through an AI-enabled decision support system. Together these loops represent $4.94M in capital investment for a 2,000-cow operation, generating $3.67M in annual net benefit and a portfolio payback of 1.3 years. Funded by USDA and dairy processors for producers and cooperatives.

INRMP Standardisation & Retro-fitting

A repeatable AI-assisted methodology for converting existing Integrated Natural Resources Management Plans into a standardised eight-chapter template. The workflow aligns existing plan content with required template expectations, identifies missing or misplaced sections, and supports quality review across sample installations from the Navy, Marine Corps, Army, and Air Force.

An Intelligent RAG Chatbot for INRMP Standardization

An agentic retrieval-augmented generation chatbot enabling semantic querying across digitized INRMPs, grounding every response in cited sources. It combines multi-mode query routing, hybrid retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, conversation persistence, knowledge graph expansion, and cloud-native storage to accelerate information discovery and cross-reference validation.

Optimizing Dairy Cattle Sustainability and Profitability Through the EcoS Index

An economic investigation into genomic selection strategy, built as a simulation model in which each animal is modeled individually and genetic progress is simulated from genomic selection data. Outputs feed a web-based decision tool that lets producers, STgenetics consultants, and policymakers analyze beef, conventional, and sexed semen breeding strategies in real time.

AI in Veterinary and Animal Science: A Book Project

A 15-chapter book — Principles, Applications, and Future Directions — authored by eight contributors from the AISLS Lab for Elsevier's ELSA platform, targeted for completion in December 2026. Chapters span foundations and data, diagnostics and imaging, precision livestock and welfare, nutrition and genetics, economics and supply chain, and ethics, education, and future techniques.
