Projects

ViCBio DM: Agent-Based Model for Circular Dairy Bioeconomy

An agent-based virtual farm modeling nutrient, water, energy, and by-product loops together — representing $4.94M in capital investment for a 2,000-cow operation, generating $3.67M in annual net benefit with a 1.3-year payback.

Agentic RAG Chatbot for Digitized INRMPs

A retrieval-augmented generation chatbot enabling semantic querying across digitized Integrated Natural Resources Management Plans (INRMPs), grounding every response in cited sources to accelerate standardization and retrofitting workflows.

Genomic Selection, Simulation Modeling & Decision-Support Strategy

An economic simulation model of genomic selection strategy, modeling each animal individually and simulating genetic progress, feeding into a web-based decision tool for producers and breeding consultants.
