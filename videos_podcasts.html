BRD Command Center: An AI Decision Aid for Feedlot Cattle

A 6-minute walkthrough of a decision aid for the first 45 days after cattle arrive. It predicts bovine respiratory disease risk for each lot, compares seven management options against current practice, plans the whole yard within capacity, and keeps antibiotic decisions with the veterinarian. Built by Team Data Ninjas for the AISLS BRD Decision Aid Hackathon, 2026.
